Global EV company

VinFast is a global electric mobility company shaping the future of transportation. Backed by Vingroup, one of Southeast Asia’s largest and most dynamic conglomerates, VinFast has rapidly become an international EV player. With manufacturing hubs in Vietnam and the United States, VinFast offers a production capacity up to 2,000 electric buses per year.

Wide range of electrical vehicles

But VinFast is more than just a vehicle manufacturer. We produce electric buses in four models, electric cars in multiple segments, and electric scooters and bikes in a wide range of configurations. Beyond production, we also operate bus systems in several major cities in Southeast Asia – giving us real-world experience with the challenges of urban mobility and public transport operations.

On the road from 100 % electric…

VinFast began as a manufacturer of internal combustion engine vehicles, but with a clear vision, a focus on sustainability, and a strong commitment to change, the company has transitioned to 100% electric.

… to 100% sustainable

Driven by green innovation for both cities and the planet, VinFast is strongly committed to the global goal of net zero emissions by 2050. With a growing ecosystem of electric products and a widespread charging network, we actively contribute to cleaner air, smarter cities, and a more sustainable transport infrastructure.

Join us on the road from 100% electrical to 100% sustainable.

Did you know?

Vietnam may be far from Europe, but it’s a country with a rapidly growing global presence. In terms of area, it is relatively large – just slightly smaller than Germany and about half the size of France.

With a population exceeding 100 million, Vietnam accounts for nearly a quarter of the EU’s population. The country is quickly expanding its global outreach and establishing strategic international collaborations.

Vietnam is currently Asia’s fastest-growing economy, with an estimated GDP growth of over 8% in 2025.

Vietnam is rising. Ride with it.

Powered by Vingroup

Vingroup is one of East Asia’s largest private conglomerates, with a bold vision to shape a modern, connected, and sustainable future.

Founded in 1993, the group has rapidly expanded across key sectors including technology, industry, real estate, healthcare, education, and mobility.

At the heart of Vingroup’s success lies a deep commitment to innovation and long-term value creation. By combining local insight with global partnerships, Vingroup delivers world-class solutions that are both scalable and impactful.

With over 50,000 employees and an expanding international footprint, the group is emerging as a rising force on the global stage. From smart cities and cutting-edge hospitals to high-performing schools and electric vehicles, Vingroup’s initiatives are all driven by one goal: improving life, in Vietnam and the world.

World-class production facilities

VinFast is building a world-class global manufacturing network to support the shift to smart and sustainable mobility.

The company’s main production hub in Hai Phong, Vietnam spans close to 1,000 hectares and ranks among the most advanced automotive factories in Southeast Asia. This fully integrated, highly automated site is designed for maximum efficiency, scalability, and high-quality output across vehicles, batteries, and components.

To serve key international markets, VinFast is expanding its global footprint with new production facilities.
